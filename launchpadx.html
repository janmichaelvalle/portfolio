SellerX LaunchpadX

An end-to-end platform that brings together the workflows required to take an e-commerce product from initial idea to marketplace launch.

RoleProduct Manager
ProductInternal operations platform
FocusWorkflow automation

Product evolution

LaunchpadX supports the complete product lifecycle: identifying new products to sell, sourcing them from suppliers, creating product records in internal systems, producing assets such as product images, researching marketplace keywords, and preparing product content for channels such as Amazon. The lifecycle ends when the finished product is published and launched in the marketplace.

This case study presents three phases that demonstrate how different parts of that lifecycle were designed and prototyped.

I built the Phase 1 prototype using Bootstrap. After validating the approach, I recommended that the company adopt v0 so team members could create and share interactive prototypes more quickly, without requiring extensive front-end development experience. Phases 2 and 3 were created using v0.
