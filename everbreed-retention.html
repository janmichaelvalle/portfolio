Everbreed — Turning Cancellation Into a Retention Opportunity

Redesigning the cancellation experience to understand why customers leave and introduce targeted retention options before they cancel.

Role
Product Manager
Focus
Retention + Churn
01

Retention problem

We Were Losing Customers Without Understanding Why

Everbreed's cancellation experience gave us limited insight into why customers were leaving and provided few opportunities to address problems that might be recoverable.

The goal wasn't to make cancellation harder. It was to understand the reason behind the decision and determine whether a relevant alternative could solve the customer's problem before they left.

Cancellation was both a churn event and an opportunity to learn.

05

Customer control

Retain When We Can. Let Customers Leave When They Want.

Retention was not implemented by making cancellation difficult. The experience balanced relevant alternatives with transparent customer choice.

Clear before the decision

  • Subscription remains active through the current billing period.
  • Premium access ends when the subscription expires.
  • Rabbit records, pedigrees, and data are retained for 6 months.
  • Users can resubscribe during the retention period.

Choice at cancellation

  • Keep Subscription
  • Contact Support
  • Cancel Subscription
  • If cancelled, show the end date and option to resume before access expires.
  • Send a confirmation email.

18.3% of cancellation attempts were retained through an alternative offered within the cancellation flow. The flow received 979 entries.

Pause emerged as the strongest retention mechanism, suggesting that many customers considering cancellation did not necessarily want to leave Everbreed permanently.
08

Product learning

What We Learned From Cancellation Behavior

  • Cancellation intent does not always mean the customer wants to leave permanently.
  • Pause was selected substantially more often than discount or downgrade.
  • Cancellation reasons can become a structured source of product, pricing, support, and competitive insight.
